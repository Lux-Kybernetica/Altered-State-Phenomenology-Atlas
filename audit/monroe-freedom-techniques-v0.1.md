# Monroe / Gateway Freedom techniques v0.1 audit

## Scope

Wave III Freedom is modeled as a procedural layer. The audit deliberately separates the technique being instructed, the phenomenology it may evoke, the transition it is intended to target, and Gateway's explanation in terms of nonphysical energy / second body. Only the first three can support Nova crosswalks.

## External concepts

Eight Freedom technique concepts are currently modeled: Liftoff, Vectors, First-Stage Separation, Log-rolling, Pole, Backing away, Flowing, and Floating. They produce **10 mappings**.

## High-confidence operational alignments

### Log-rolling → Rotation volontaire

Gateway explicitly instructs deliberate axial rotation, then advises stopping the forcing once the rotation/release is established and allowing the process to continue outward. Nova `Rotation volontaire` is likewise an intentional use/amplification of rotation in a separation context.

Mapping confidence: **0.98**.

### First-Stage Separation → Décrochage

The exercise is explicitly designed around methods of dissociation/separation. Nova `Décrochage` models the transition rather than the destination.

Mapping confidence: **0.97**.

### Vectors → Orientation

Vectors teaches repeatable body-referenced directional points and controlled movement. Nova `Orientation` is the action of choosing a direction, target or intentional reference.

Mapping confidence: **0.94**.

## Important distinction: Liftoff

The Gateway manual explicitly states that Liftoff's floating-away perspective does **not necessarily imply actual separation**.

Therefore two mappings are retained simultaneously: Liftoff `technique_targets` Nova `Flottement`, while Liftoff also `distinguish_from` Nova `Décrochage`.

This prevents a training cue/rehearsal from being counted as a separation transition.

## Partial-exit structure: Pole

Gateway's Pole method describes progressive displacement with the feet remaining the final pivot/contact point before intended complete separation. This is a strong procedural analogue for a path through Nova `Sortie partielle` toward `Décrochage`, but it is not evidence that spontaneous partial exits follow the same body-part order.

## No-equivalent procedure: Flowing

Gateway's cloud/fog/spray-outflow visualization has no direct Nova action concept. It is therefore recorded as `no_direct_equivalent` at the procedure level while still `technique_targets` `nova.decrochage` at the transition-goal level.

This is intentional and demonstrates why procedure and target must remain distinct axes.

## Source-claim boundary

Fifteen typed Freedom claims have been added:

- 9 `procedural_instruction` records eligible for crosswalk comparison;
- 4 `interpretive_claim` records;
- 2 `metaphysical_claim` records.

Interpretations involving nonphysical energy, out-of-phase energies, or a second body are retained as Gateway source framing and are **not imported as Nova facts**.

## Result

**PASS at manual review — 8 Freedom technique concepts, 10 non-identity mappings, and 15 typed source claims preserve procedure, phenomenology, transition target and theory as separate layers.**
