# NC × Lux — Review batch 7: Safety

## Scope

Manual review of all **9 Nova safety entries**.

## Main finding
The existing multi-type model is sufficient. Safety entries separate into:

- preventive concepts (`Hygiène de pratique`, `Cadre de prudence`);
- adjustment actions (`Dosage`, `Pause stratégique`);
- post-experience actions (`Ancrage post-expérience`, `Intégration`);
- warning conditions (`Dérégulation du sommeil`, `Anxiété persistante`, `Confusion réalité/rêve`).

No additional `safety_role` axis is required yet because these distinctions are already expressible through `types[]`, phases, cognitive dimensions and action functions.

## Epistemic caution

Safety recommendations are preserved as Nova operational guidance, not silently promoted into medical or clinical standards.

Examples:

- the source recommendation to pause if sleep disruption lasts more than a week is stored as an interpretation/recommendation rather than a universal clinical threshold;
- anxiety and reality/dream confusion are represented as Nova warning conditions without assigning diagnosis or etiology.
