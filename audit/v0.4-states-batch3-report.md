## Main modelling result

The family exposed a structural weakness in the previous model: the modality `cognitive` was too coarse. Nova already distinguishes several cognitive functions that should remain separate.

A new optional axis `cognitive_dimensions[]` was therefore added with the controlled values:

- `meta_awareness`
- `reasoning_clarity`
- `memory_continuity`
- `goal_maintenance`
- `executive_control`
- `attention_stability`
- `temporal_continuity`
- `reality_monitoring`

This axis identifies *which cognitive function is involved* without yet forcing a universal severity scale.

## Key distinctions preserved

### Lucidité vs Clarté mentale
Nova treats these as distinct. Lucidity involves knowing one is in the experience and retaining some capacity for action; mental clarity concerns quality of reasoning/discernment.

### Conscience de soi vs Pilotage automatique
Self-awareness is represented primarily through `meta_awareness`; pilotage automatique through reduced `agency`, `executive_control` and `goal_maintenance` while consciousness remains present.

### Semi-lucidité vs Lucidité intermittente
These should not be collapsed into one scalar value. Semi-lucidité describes partial lucidity; lucidité intermittente specifically describes repeated temporal alternation between lucid observation and immersion.

### Lucidité continue
Requires `temporal_continuity` and `memory_continuity`, because Nova explicitly defines it across multiple phases rather than as merely high-quality lucidity at one moment.

### Rêve lucide
Adds `reality_monitoring`: the subject identifies the environment as a dream. Nova's rule distinguishing lucid dream and SHC by environmental malléability/stability is preserved as an internal source criterion, not upgraded to an externally validated fact.

### Rappel stable
Modelled as a post-experience memory/temporal-continuity condition, not as a measure of experiential intensity.
