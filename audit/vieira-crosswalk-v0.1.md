# Vieira / Projeciologia × Nova — crosswalk v0.1 audit

## Scope

This audit covers the first source-grounded Projectiology crosswalk built from direct/institutionally hosted Vieira-framework material rather than relying on Nova's existing parenthetical Vieira labels.

## Coverage

- External Projectiology concepts: **7**
- Crosswalk mappings: **7**
- Identity mappings: **0**
- `no_direct_equivalent` decisions: **1** (`ballonnement`)
- Canonical NC references unresolved after manual review: **0**

## Strong operational correspondences

### Estado vibracional → État vibratoire

Strong phenomenological overlap: both describe intense/internal vibration phenomena in a transition/separation context.

The Projectiology explanation in terms of energossoma and psicossoma is **not** imported.

### Semiprojeção → Sortie partielle

Strong structural overlap. IIPC describes partial exteriorization using limb-by-limb examples; Nova describes incomplete exit-like dissociation in which only part of the perceived body seems free.

The framework explanation (partial psychosome exteriorization) remains external.

### Catalepsia projetiva → Paralysie du sommeil

Strong operational overlap around temporary physical immobility with awareness present. The Projectiology vehicle-coincidence explanation is not imported as the mechanism of Nova's concept.

## Moderate / deliberately non-identical correspondences

### Decolagem do psicossoma → Décrochage

The transition structure overlaps strongly, but the concepts differ ontologically. Projectiology names takeoff of the framework-defined psychosome; Nova defines a perceptual shift / separation of the center of perception and explicitly treats Décrochage as passage rather than destination.

### Psicossoma → Corps de sortie

Only a **partial overlap** is accepted.

Nova explicitly defines Corps de sortie as a perception of a different/light/fluid body and explicitly warns that this is not ontological proof. Projectiology treats Psicossoma as a vehicle of manifestation. Mapping identity would erase precisely the epistemic distinction Lux was designed to preserve.

### Projeção semiconsciente → Semi-lucidité + Rêve lucide

Projectiology material describes semiconscious projection through partial/discontinuous lucidity and also places it near lucid-dream terminology. Nova deliberately separates degree of lucidity from the environmental category of lucid dream, so the external concept maps as a partial overlap with two NC nodes rather than collapsing them.

## No-equivalent decision

### Ballonnement

Projectiology describes a specific swelling/inflation or expansion-of-body sensation around pre-takeoff. Nova currently has no canonical node specific enough to represent it.

It is therefore recorded as `no_direct_equivalent` rather than being forced into Flottement or Dissolution des frontières.

## Source / ontology boundary

Projectiology/IIPC source material describes psicossoma, energossoma, holossoma, extraphysical dimensions and vehicle disconnection as features of its consciousness paradigm. These are retained as source-framework claims only.

Phenomenological and procedural descriptions may support crosswalk mappings without accepting those explanatory entities as established facts.
