# Monroe / Gateway Focus ladder v0.1 audit

## Scope

The Focus ladder is modeled as Gateway-specific program terminology. It is not a universal sequence of consciousness states and is not imported as Nova ontology.

## Focus concepts reviewed

- Focus 3
- Focus 10
- Focus 12
- Focus 15
- Focus 21
- Focus 27

## Mapping decisions

### Focus 3 — no direct equivalent

Gateway defines Focus 3 as a coherent/synchronized/balanced mind-brain condition. Nova has no canonical unit with the same operational definition.

Decision: `no_direct_equivalent`.

### Focus 10 — operational overlap

Gateway's mind-awake/body-asleep state has a strong operational resemblance to Nova `Endormissement conscient`, but the former is a program target state and the latter a phenomenological process/transition.

Decision: `operationally_similar_to`, never identity.

### Focus 12 — no direct equivalent

Gateway's expanded-awareness label is intentionally broad and tied to a wide range of exercises and source-specific interpretations. Mapping it to generic Nova `Lucidité` would falsely collapse breadth of awareness, self-awareness and lucidity into one concept.

Decision: `no_direct_equivalent`.

### Focus 15 — partial temporal overlap only

Gateway defines Focus 15 as a 'no time' condition / stillness or simply being. Nova `Distorsion du temps` provides a limited phenomenological comparison for altered subjective temporality.

The separate Gateway claim that an energy body is not limited by time and space is excluded from the mapping.

Decision: `partial_overlap` with `nova.distorsion_du_temps`.

### Focus 21 — no direct equivalent

Gateway defines Focus 21 as a bridge to other energy/reality systems. This is program/cosmology-specific and has no direct NC concept.

Decision: `no_direct_equivalent`.

### Focus 27 — no direct equivalent

Voyager defines Focus 27 as a reception center between physical lives. This is an external interpretive/cosmological construct and is outside the present Nova phenomenological ontology.

Decision: `no_direct_equivalent`.

## Source-claim separation

The same manuals can contain several epistemically different statements next to each other. The new source-claim layer therefore distinguishes:

- `program_definition`
- `procedural_instruction`
- `participant_report_generalization`
- `interpretive_claim`
- `metaphysical_claim`

For example, 'Focus 15 is the state of no time' is recorded as a Gateway program definition; the claim that an energy body exists outside time/space is recorded separately as a metaphysical source claim.

## Result

**PASS at the manual data-model level — six Focus levels modeled, one strong operational overlap, one narrow partial overlap, four explicit no-equivalent decisions, and zero identity mappings.**
