# Nova × Monroe × Vieira convergence matrix — manual audit v0.1

## Scope

The matrix compares committed external crosswalks against canonical Nova concepts. It is designed to identify reusable phenomenological or procedural minima while preserving theoretical disagreement.

**Convergence is not a truth vote.** Two traditions describing similar experiences do not independently establish the reality of either tradition's proposed mechanism or ontology.

## Current matrix

- NC convergence clusters: **8**
- Unique external concepts available across current crosswalks: **32**
- Clusters with at least two external traditions: **3**

## True multi-tradition convergence targets

### 1. État vibratoire

External alignments:

- Monroe/Gateway perceived vibrations → `nova.etat_vibratoire` (0.90)
- Vieira/Projectiology estado vibracional → `nova.etat_vibratoire` (0.95)

Shared minimum: perceived internal/body-wide vibration, oscillation, electric or shaking-like sensation in a transition/separation context.

Divergence: Gateway uses nonphysical-energy training language; Projectiology explains the phenomenon through energossoma/psychosome dynamics; Nova can remain phenomenological.

**Research value: high.**

### 2. Décrochage

External alignments include:

- Monroe/Gateway First-Stage Separation → targets `nova.decrochage` (0.97)
- Monroe/Gateway separation goal → partial overlap (0.72)
- Vieira/Projectiology decolagem do psicossoma → `nova.decrochage` (0.87)

Shared minimum: transition away from ordinary bodily localization toward a separation-like or displaced point of view, distinct from the later destination/outcome.

Divergence: what is said to separate differs sharply: Gateway physical/nonphysical energies or second body; Vieira psychosome; Nova center-of-perception/self-location transition.

**Research value: high.**

### 3. Sortie partielle

External alignments:

- Monroe/Gateway Pole method → targets `nova.sortie_partielle` (0.89)
- Vieira/Projectiology semiprojection → `nova.sortie_partielle` (0.94)

Shared minimum: incomplete separation-like embodiment in which only part of the experienced body representation is displaced/free before possible completion.

Divergence: Gateway second-body technique, Projectiology psychosome exteriorization, Nova perceived partial-body transition.

**Research value: high.**

## Strong single-tradition redundancy

This is strong procedural redundancy **inside Gateway**, not independent cross-tradition confirmation.

### Flottement

Gateway contributes kinesthetic motion cues, Liftoff and the Floating separation method. Again, this is one tradition using several exercises/cue families around a similar phenomenology.

## Epistemically important single alignments

### Paralysie du sommeil ↔ Catalepsia projetiva

Strong Vieira operational correspondence (0.93). Gateway discusses temporary stiffness/immobility in Freedom, but no dedicated Gateway concept has yet been formalized, so a second-tradition alignment is deliberately not counted.

### Corps de sortie ↔ Psicossoma

Weak partial overlap (0.58) with major ontological divergence. This cluster is valuable precisely because Nova explicitly stays at the level of perceived embodiment while Projectiology treats Psicossoma as a real vehicle inside its framework.

### Lâcher-prise ↔ Gateway nonattachment

Strong operational alignment (0.94) with repeated support inside Gateway Freedom, but still only one external tradition at present.

## Result

**PASS at manual review — the matrix identifies three genuine multi-tradition comparison targets while explicitly preventing within-tradition repetition or shared metaphysical language from masquerading as independent confirmation.**
