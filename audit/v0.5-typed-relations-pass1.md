## Review policy applied

- A lexical resonance is never accepted merely because the terms are connected in Nova v1.
- Confusion entries are inspected semantically; they are not blindly converted to `distinguish_from`.
- Temporal wording remains weaker than causality.
- `facilitates` / `inhibits` encode operational effect claims without upgrading them to causal laws.
- One lexical pair may legitimately produce more than one semantic edge. Example: `Flottement` is distinguished from `Décrochage`, but Nova also says it can precede or accompany it.
- Pathway hypotheses remain outside this file.

## Important correction to automation

The initial machine heuristic over-detected generic terms such as `Lucidité` inside compounds like `Semi-lucidité` or `Lucidité intermittente`. The candidate generator was tightened before its output could be treated as review evidence.
