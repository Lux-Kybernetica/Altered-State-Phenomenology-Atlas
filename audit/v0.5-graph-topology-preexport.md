## Interpretation

The centrality pattern is already meaningful: the source-derived semantic network concentrates around a **cognitive hub** (`Lucidité`) and a **transition hub** (`Zone de bascule`), with `Endormissement` and `Décrochage` acting as major process pivots.

The 28 isolated nodes are **not automatically defects**. They are concepts for which Nova v1 supplied no lexical resonance that survived semantic review. They must remain isolated until another justified relation source exists (internal definition review, external literature, structured experience data, or a separate pathway model).

This audit intentionally does not invent edges merely to make the visualization prettier.
